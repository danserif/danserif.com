[About]

Kia ora. Hello. I’m Dan Newman, an experienced design leader and designer from Wellington, New Zealand.

Over the past two decades I’ve led, hired, and managed growing teams in several startups and digital agencies. This work was across timezones, in offices, and remotely, all while continuing to design and direct projects. I love working with fun, genuine, creative people, while helping organisations with a real purpose and vision.

[Skills]

I take great pride in supporting creative teams to do their best work with an empathetic and encouraging leadership style. In over two decades working hands-on and directing teams, I’ve designed everything from websites, apps, installations, and design systems to conferences, keynotes, swag, office fitouts, posters, billboards, advertising campaigns, logos, and brands.

I’m experienced in the following areas:

  • — Visual design, art direction, and typography
  • — Branding, identity design, and design systems
  • — Advertising, marketing, and growth experimentation
  • — User research, usability testing, and prototyping
  • — Managing multidisciplinary teams and projects
  • — Remote and hybrid team management
  • — Leading design ideation and the creative process
  • — Front-end development support and collaboration
  • — Shipping and refining quality user experiences
  • — AI-assisted design and development
